Hi, my name is Alessandro Zanella.
Welcome To my site, where i share my dance knowledge!

The salsa taught here is my personal style, rooted in the global evolution of salsa, which began with traditional Cuban salsa and has grown through diverse regional interpretations. It’s practical salsa, widely danced at festivals and events, but reflects my unique approach, shaped by years of learning from and blending influences from others. Salsa is a living art, not a single truth, and my system is just one way to enjoy it. The information provided may be incomplete or incorrect; if you notice anything off, please share your feedback through the contact form linked on the website.
