# Explaining The Levels - Beginner | Inter | Advanced
Difficulty Depends quantity and variety of fundamental techniques and the speed of the song.

Combos fall roughly in these three categories. Combos usually consiste of multiple smaller combos in varying difficulties. A long combo can always be broken down into multiple small ones consisting of multiple [[Salsa/2 - Salsa Fundamentals/index|Fundamental Techniques]]

Here is my rough definition of the 3 levels.
### Beginner:
max 1 - 3 techniques per 8 count
and/or
< 110 BPM
### Intermediate:
4 -6 techniques per 8 count
and/or
100-160 BPM
### Advanced: 
\> 6 techniques per 8 count
and/or
\> 160 BPM
