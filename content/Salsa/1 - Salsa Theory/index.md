---
title: 1 - Salsa Theory
---

People underestimate how useful theory is for dancing. If you understand the theory behind the dance, it will no longer require you to think very hard about certain actions because they will simply become logical conclusions.

e.g. Stepping Follower Basic and switching with two slows(2 beats x2) to re-enter Leader Basic for partnerwork, instead of stopping the dance counting and then restarting.

If that doesnt mean anything to you no worries. It will in the future.

The best way to learn theory in my opinion is to train theory with practical examples to make it intuitive.

Being able to intuitively apply theory practically will get you to the advanced level. Without it, you will struggle.
