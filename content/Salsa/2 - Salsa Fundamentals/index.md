# Salsa Social Dancing is 100% fundamentals
in order to truly dance salsa, you don't need to learn long combinations but the building blocks for those combinations. These building blocks i call fundamentals.

When you learn the fundamentals you will not need to think about combinations and what comes next but you will be able to simply feel the music and come up with them on the spot, thats the ultimate goal and what i am aiming for with my teachings.
